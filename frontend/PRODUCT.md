# FitConnect — Product Context

## Product Purpose
FitConnect is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for gym management. It enables gym owners and staff to manage members, payments, check-ins, classes, equipment, and reports from a single dashboard.

## Register
product

## Users
- **Gym Owners** (primary): Business owners managing 1-5 gyms. Care about revenue, member retention, operational efficiency. Use the app daily at a desk or reception area.
- **Admins**: Senior staff with full access except billing. Use it throughout the day on desktop.
- **Receptionists**: Front-desk staff managing check-ins and member lookup. Use it in bursts at the reception desk.

## Brand Tone
Professional but energetic. Confident without being aggressive. The gym world expects strength and clarity — not corporate blandness.

## Anti-References
- Generic SaaS beige (Notion-style cream with tiny Volt accents)
- Dark "hacker" aesthetic (terminal green on black)
- Fitness-bro loudness (neon colors everywhere, aggressive gradients)
- Medical/clinical white (sterile, no personality)

## Strategic Principles
- Volt is the identity color — use it with commitment, not as a 10% accent
- Every screen should feel fast and purposeful — no decorative filler
- Data must be readable at a glance — hierarchy before aesthetics
- The app runs on desktop in well-lit gym environments — light mode is default
