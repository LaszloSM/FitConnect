# FitConnect — Product Context

## Product Purpose
SaaS platform for gym management targeting the Colombian market. Gym owners use it daily to manage members, payments, classes, check-ins, and machines. Receptionists use it for rapid member check-in and payment registration. The product competes with Mindbody, Glofox, and Perfect Gym.

## Register
product

## Users
- **Gym owners** (primary): review KPIs, manage staff, configure gym settings, see revenue. Moderate-to-advanced tech literacy.
- **Receptionists** (high-frequency): fast member check-in, register payments, quick lookups. Low tech literacy, speed is critical.
- **Admins**: manage member plans, classes, machines.

## Brand
- **Name:** FitConnect
- **Color:** Orange (oklch ~0.70 0.20 47) as brand primary accent — kept as identity, not overused.
- **Tone:** Professional, trustworthy, energetic. Not casual. Not startup-cute. Like a tool a serious gym owner pays for.
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans (already set up) — clean, modern, readable.

## Anti-references
- Mindbody (cluttered, outdated, overwhelming)
- Generic SaaS cream (#f9f9f9 + blue)
- Fitness app clichés (neon green, black backgrounds with muscle imagery)
- Over-animated dashboards

## Strategic principles
- Speed over decoration: receptionist workflow must be 2-3 clicks max
- Data density: owners need KPIs at a glance, not buried under cards
- Dark mode is first-class, not an afterthought
- Every form field must serve a real gym workflow need
- Colombian market context: COP currency, CC/NIT documents, Wompi payments
